# Pricing, markets, discounts, product types, quantity limits and platform terms for a vitrine-store -> checkout-store middleware (Shopify, researched 2026-10-05, latest stable API 2026-10)

Researched on 2026-10-05 from Shopify primary documentation. Each claim was re-checked by an independent verifier;
the verdict is shown in brackets. "CORRECTION" lines override the claim they follow. Items marked community/RISK are not official.

## Claims

- **PT-00** [confirmed, confidence high] The latest stable API version is 2026-10 (released October 1, 2026, accessible until October 16, 2027 15:00 UTC). 2027-01 is the release candidate. The Storefront API reference pages under /latest/ render as version 2026-10.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/usage/versioning
- **PT-01** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] The Storefront cart's `cost` is derived from the cart's `buyerIdentity`; prices are computed by the store that owns the cart (the checkout store), not supplied by the client. CartLineInput has no price field (fields: attributes, merchandiseId, quantity, sellingPlanId, parent).
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest/objects/Cart
- **PT-02** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] CartBuyerIdentityInput.countryCode (type CountryCode, 'The country where the buyer is located.') drives international pricing and is expected to match the shipping address. CartInput.buyerIdentity is described as 'The customer associated with the cart. Used to determine international pricing.'
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest/objects/CartBuyerIdentity
- **PT-03** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] The international pricing guide states that the country code in buyerIdentity contextualizes the cart's estimated cost, and that buyerIdentity also ensures products are published for that country.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/building-with-the-storefront-api/markets/international-pricing
- **PT-04** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Without the @inContext directive, the Storefront API returns products published for the primary market, priced in the shop's currency.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/building-with-the-storefront-api/markets/international-pricing
- **PT-05** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] A country context that is not enabled on the store falls back to the store currency.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/building-with-the-storefront-api/markets/international-pricing
- **PT-06** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Each country's currency must be enabled in the store's payment settings before queries in that country context return local pricing. `localization` on QueryRoot lists available countries and currencies.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/building-with-the-storefront-api/markets/international-pricing
- **PT-07** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] The international pricing / cart flow lists these unauthenticated scopes as requirements: unauthenticated_read_product_listings, unauthenticated_read_customers, unauthenticated_write_checkouts. In the access-scopes table, unauthenticated_read_checkouts / unauthenticated_write_checkouts grant access to `Cart`.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/building-with-the-storefront-api/markets/international-pricing
- **PT-08** [partially_correct, design-critical, confidence medium] @inContext takes an optional country code (also language, buyer, visitorConsent) and applies it to a query or mutation. In 2026-10 it additionally accepts an optional `channelId` that applies a sales channel's product availability and pricing.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/changelog/release-notes/2026-10
  **CORRECTION:** channelId in 2026-10 is confirmed (changelog dated July 15, 2026). Two things are understated. (1) Argument list per the contextual-queries guide is country, subdivision, language, buyer, visitorConsent, channelId (plus preferredLocationId for pickup). (2) On carts @inContext country does NOT reliably apply: "After a buyer identity is set with cartBuyerIdentityUpdate or during cartCreate, the buyer identity takes precedence" and conflicting values such as country "are ignored"; language, visitorConsent and channelId keep applying. The pricing guide says "cart uses an explicit buyerIdentity argument". So set the country through CartInput.buyerIdentity.countryCode, not '@inContext and/or buyerIdentity'. channelId must belong to the same store and same app as the credentials, otherwise error "Channel not found for the given id." (https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/building-with-the-storefront-api/in-context)
- **PT-09** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Market/catalog prices come from a PriceList, which overrides the default variant price with fixed prices or a percentage adjustment (PriceListParent). PriceList.currency is 'The currency for fixed prices associated with this price list.'
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/objects/PriceList
- **PT-10** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] A fixed PriceListPrice for a variant takes precedence over price-list-level percentage adjustments.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/markets/catalogs-different-markets
- **PT-11** [partially_correct, design-critical, confidence high] When a fixed price-list price applies, neither FX conversion nor rounding is applied. This is the only documented way to get a deterministic local-currency price.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/markets/catalogs-different-markets
  **CORRECTION:** First sentence confirmed verbatim (fixed price-list price => no FX, no rounding). The second sentence ('the only documented way to get a deterministic local-currency price') is stronger than the docs: manual exchange rates are also documented as a stabilising mechanism - "With manual exchange rates, you set a fixed exchange rate for each currency." (help.shopify.com/en/manual/markets/customizations/local-currencies). The exchange-rates page adds that manual rates cannot be applied to the primary market. Fixed prices remain the only route documented as skipping both FX and rounding. (https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/markets/catalogs-different-markets)
- **PT-12** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] A market's currency and its price list currency must match; when a buyer is eligible for several catalogs the lowest price wins.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/markets/catalogs-different-markets
- **PT-13** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Automatic conversion formula: product price x conversion rate, plus conversion fee, then rounding rules if enabled. Documented example: ($10.00 USD x 0.867519) x (1 + .015) = EUR 8.81.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/pricing/exchange-rates
- **PT-14** [partially_correct, design-critical, confidence high] Auto-converted prices move with exchange rates; rates are 'updated regularly throughout the day' (no exact frequency documented). Manual exchange rates per market are possible.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/pricing/exchange-rates
  **CORRECTION:** The quoted sentence "The prices in your store change automatically with market exchange rates." is on the cited page, but the phrase 'updated regularly throughout the day' is NOT on that page; it is on help.shopify.com/en/manual/markets/customizations/local-currencies: "Rates are sourced from financial markets and updated regularly throughout the day." Also 'no exact frequency documented' is wrong: the Admin REST Currency resource states "Conversion rates are checked every 15 minutes, but typically updated only a few times a day." Manual rates exist but (per the exchange-rates page) cannot be applied to the primary market. (https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-rest/latest/resources/currency)
- **PT-15** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Rounding rules, when active, round converted prices to a per-currency default and cannot be customized; they do not apply to gift cards.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/pricing/rounding
- **PT-16** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence medium] A currency conversion fee (1.5% or 2% depending on store location, for Shopify Payments) is built into the converted price shown to the customer.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/pricing/fees
- **PT-17** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Charging in local currencies requires Shopify Payments or Adyen; with any other provider orders are processed in the store's default currency. Local currencies are supported in headless/Storefront API.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/pricing/limitations
- **PT-18** [confirmed, confidence high] Local-currency support explicitly covers headless storefronts built on the Storefront API.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/pricing/limitations
- **PT-19** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] If the buyer ships to a country different from the one used for pricing, checkout changes the currency (and prices) to match the shipping destination and notifies the buyer. Applies to stores with one-page checkout and physical goods.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/pricing/checkout-restrictions
- **PT-20** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Dynamic tax-inclusive pricing changes the displayed/charged price by buyer region (tax included or stripped). Fixed per-market prices are NOT adjusted.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/pricing/dynamic-tax-inclusive-pricing
- **PT-21** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Cart costs are estimates; the authoritative amounts are those at checkout. CartCost fields: checkoutChargeAmount, subtotalAmount, subtotalAmountEstimated, totalAmount, totalAmountEstimated (totalTaxAmount/totalDutyAmount deprecated).
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest/objects/CartCost
- **PT-22** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Admin API: ProductVariant.price is the shop-currency price only; market-specific price must be read with ProductVariant.contextualPricing(context: ...). ProductVariantContextualPricing.price is 'The final price after all adjustments are applied.' 2026-10 adds `auditTrail` listing the ordered price adjustments.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/objects/ProductVariant
- **PT-23** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] In Markets catalogs (admin), a manually set product price overrides the catalog's overall price adjustment, and customers pay in their market's currency regardless of catalog currency.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/markets-new/catalogs
- **PT-24** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] A catalog's publication restricts which products buyers in that market can view AND purchase. Storefront cart warning code PRODUCT_UNAVAILABLE_IN_BUYER_LOCATION exists ('The product is unavailable in the current buyer location.').
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/markets/catalogs-different-markets
- **PT-25** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence medium] The vitrine's /cart.js returns money values in the buyer's presentment currency (in cents), so vitrine cart prices are not comparable across currencies and must not be forwarded.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/ajax/reference/cart
- **PT-30** [confirmed, confidence high] CartInput.discountCodes is [String!], case-insensitive, max 250 values. Other CartInput fields: attributes, buyerIdentity, delivery, giftCardCodes, lines, metafields, note, sourceName.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest/input-objects/CartInput
- **PT-31** [confirmed, confidence high] Cart.discountCodes returns CartDiscountCode objects with fields `code` and `applicable` (Boolean).
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest/objects/CartDiscountCode
- **PT-32** [confirmed, confidence high] Since Storefront API 2025-07, non-applicable discount codes also produce CartWarning entries explaining why; before that the only signal was applicable: false.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/changelog/posts/add-warnings-for-discount-codes
- **PT-33** [partially_correct, confidence medium] A code that does not exist on the checkout store maps to CartWarningCode DISCOUNT_NOT_FOUND. Other discount warning codes: DISCOUNT_CODE_NOT_HONOURED, DISCOUNT_CURRENTLY_INACTIVE, DISCOUNT_CUSTOMER_NOT_ELIGIBLE, DISCOUNT_CUSTOMER_USAGE_LIMIT_REACHED, DISCOUNT_ELIGIBLE_CUSTOMER_MISSING, DISCOUNT_INCOMPATIBLE_PURCHASE_TYPE, DISCOUNT_NO_ENTITLED_LINE_ITEMS, DISCOUNT_NO_ENTITLED_SHIPPING_LINES, DISCOUNT_PURCHASE_NOT_IN_RANGE, DISCOUNT_QUANTITY_NOT_IN_RANGE, DISCOUNT_USAGE_LIMIT_REACHED. No worked example of an unknown code is documented; the combination (applicable:false + DISCOUNT_NOT_FOUND warning, mutation succeeds) is inferred from the enum and changelog.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest/enums/CartWarningCode
  **CORRECTION:** All twelve DISCOUNT_* enum values and their descriptions are confirmed for 2026-10. What is not confirmed is the behavioural mapping: no primary page shows that a code absent from the store yields applicable:false plus a DISCOUNT_NOT_FOUND warning with a successful mutation. The cartCreate reference page exposes no such example. Treat it as an inference to be tested against a dev store, exactly as the researcher flagged. (https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest/enums/CartWarningCode)
- **PT-34** [confirmed, confidence high] Warnings are non-blocking: the mutation succeeds. CartWarning fields are `code`, `message` (localized), `target` (ID of the affected cart object). Payloads of cartCreate return `cart`, `userErrors: [CartUserError!]!`, `warnings: [CartWarning!]!`.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/building-with-the-storefront-api/cart/cart-warnings
- **PT-35** [confirmed, confidence high] cartDiscountCodesUpdate(cartId, discountCodes: [String!]!) replaces the whole code list (max 250 values); an empty array removes all codes.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest/mutations/cartDiscountCodesUpdate
- **PT-36** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Automatic discounts configured on the checkout store apply in cart and checkout without any input, on all sales channels. Cart exposes them as CartAutomaticDiscountAllocation.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/discounts/sales-channel-discounts
- **PT-37** [confirmed, confidence high] Discount codes cannot be restricted to particular sales channels; a code created on the checkout store is redeemable from a Storefront API/custom-app channel too.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/discounts/sales-channel-discounts
- **PT-38** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Per-order code limit: 5 product/order codes plus 1 shipping code.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/discounts/discount-combinations
- **PT-39** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Maximum 25 active automatic discounts per store, including app (Functions) discounts.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/discounts/discount-combinations
- **PT-40** [confirmed, confidence high] When applied discounts cannot combine (combination settings or cart contents), Shopify applies the best one for the buyer. Combining product+order or stacking order discounts requires no checkout.liquid; multiple product discounts on the same line item is Shopify Plus only.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/discounts/discount-combinations
- **PT-41** [confirmed, confidence medium] Shopify Functions discounts run server-side inside Shopify when buyers add products to their cart; discount classes are Order, Product, Shipping. Cart exposes them as CartCustomDiscountAllocation.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/discounts
- **PT-42** [confirmed, confidence high] Functions in a custom app (the kind this middleware would own) require Shopify Plus; public App Store apps with Functions work on any plan.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/functions
- **PT-43** [confirmed, confidence high] As of 2026-07, cart.discountAllocations is deprecated in favour of cart.lines[].discountAllocations(lineLevelOnly: false) and cart.deliveryGroups[].discountAllocations; `lineLevelOnly` defaults to true; new cart.discountApplications lists all discounts; use sourceDiscountApplication instead of discountApplication.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/changelog/new-discount-fields-in-the-storefront-cart-graphql-api
- **PT-44** [confirmed, confidence medium] Fixed-amount discounts are converted to the buyer's currency at checkout and fluctuate with exchange rates.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/pricing/discounts
- **PT-45** [confirmed, confidence high] Fallback cart permalink supports a `discount` query parameter; multiple codes are comma-separated, so codes containing a comma cannot be passed.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/checkout/create-cart-permalinks
- **PT-50** [confirmed, confidence high] Subscriptions on a cart line use CartLineInput.sellingPlanId (ID). Related CartErrorCode values: SELLING_PLAN_NOT_APPLICABLE ('Selling plan is not applicable.') and VARIANT_REQUIRES_SELLING_PLAN ('Variant can only be purchased with a selling plan.').
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest/input-objects/CartLineInput
- **PT-51** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Reading selling plans through the Storefront API requires the unauthenticated_read_selling_plans scope, and subscription purchases must go through the cart workflow.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/building-with-the-storefront-api/products-collections/subscriptions
- **PT-52** [confirmed, confidence medium] The checkout store must use a supported gateway for subscriptions (listed: Shopify Payments, PayPal Express, Authorize.net, Adyen, Stripe). Gift cards on subscriptions apply only to the first payment.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/purchase-options/subscriptions/considerations
- **PT-53** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] The cart-permalink fallback cannot carry selling plans. Permalink format: https://{shop}.myshopify.com/cart/#{variant_id}:#{quantity}(,...). Supported params include discount, checkout[email], note, attributes, ref, access_token, storefront, payment=shop_pay.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/checkout/create-cart-permalinks
- **PT-54** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Gift cards are strictly store-specific: a card issued on the vitrine cannot be redeemed on the checkout store.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/gift-card-products/overview
- **PT-55** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] There is no native transfer of gift cards between stores.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/gift-card-products/overview
- **PT-56** [confirmed, confidence high] CartInput.giftCardCodes is [String!], case-insensitive, max 250 values; cartGiftCardCodesAdd(cartId, giftCardCodes: [String!]!) appends codes. Warning code PAYMENTS_GIFT_CARDS_UNAVAILABLE = 'Gift cards are not available as a payment method.'
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest/mutations/cartGiftCardCodesAdd
- **PT-57** [confirmed, confidence medium] Gift card currency rule: store-currency cards redeem in any currency; local-currency cards only when checkout currency matches.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/gift-card-products/overview
- **PT-58** [partially_correct, design-critical, confidence medium] Bundles are supported on Storefront API carts (fixed and customized), but bundle publishing must be enabled for custom storefronts. Bundles cannot be combined with selling plans.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/product-merchandising/bundles
  **CORRECTION:** Support and the selling-plan exclusion are confirmed ("Bundles can't be sold with selling plans, such as subscriptions, pre-orders, and try-before-you-buy."). 'Publishing must be enabled' hides conditions that matter for a Dev Dashboard app: the turn-on-publishing page says publishing is disabled by default for custom storefronts, the app must be converted to a sales channel and the 'Sell Bundles' feature enabled in the Partner Dashboard, and only product fixed bundles (not variant fixed bundles) can be published. Exception: "On admin-created custom apps, publishing bundles is enabled by default". Whether a Dev Dashboard app counts as 'admin-created' is not documented. (https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/product-merchandising/bundles/turn-on-publishing)
- **PT-59** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] A variant with requiresComponents = true can only be bought as a bundle parent and is omitted from channels without bundle support (affects visibility of the mapped variant on the checkout store's Storefront channel).
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/objects/ProductVariant
- **PT-60** [confirmed, confidence medium] Cart Transform Function API operations are lineExpand, linesMerge, lineUpdate. lineUpdate (price/title/image override) is limited to development stores and Shopify Plus. Operations are rejected when a selling plan is present. Cart error code MERCHANDISE_LINE_TRANSFORMERS_RUN_ERROR = 'An error occurred while processing cart transformations.'
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/functions/latest/cart-transform
- **PT-61** [confirmed, confidence high] Shopify Bundles eligibility: upgraded checkout (no checkout.liquid), Online Store channel or custom storefront, a bundles app installed; bundle inventory is bounded by the scarcest component; draft orders may not support bundles.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/products/bundles/eligibility-and-considerations
- **PT-62** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Draft orders - price override on a catalog variant: DraftOrderLineItemInput.priceOverride (MoneyInput, presentment currency) replaces the variant's catalog price for that draft order; converted if currency differs; cannot apply to bundle components. generatePriceOverride (Boolean) creates an override from the current price.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/input-objects/DraftOrderLineItemInput
- **PT-63** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Draft orders - custom line items: when variantId is null, title, originalUnitPriceWithCurrency (MoneyInput), sku, taxable, requiresShipping, weight define the item. All of those are ignored when variantId is provided (originalUnitPrice is deprecated).
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/input-objects/DraftOrderLineItemInput
- **PT-64** [confirmed, confidence high] Draft orders - discounts: appliedDiscount (DraftOrderAppliedDiscountInput: value Float!, valueType DraftOrderAppliedDiscountType!, title, description, amountWithCurrency) at line and order level; DraftOrderInput also has discountCodes, acceptAutomaticDiscounts, allowDiscountCodesInCheckout, shippingLine, taxExempt, presentmentCurrencyCode, reserveInventoryUntil.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/input-objects/DraftOrderAppliedDiscountInput
- **PT-65** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] draftOrderCreate requires write_draft_orders (or write_quick_sale); line item cap is 499 since API 2025-07 (was 250). DraftOrder.invoiceUrl is 'The link to the checkout, which is sent to the customer in the invoice email.'
  Source: https://shopify.dev/changelog/draft-order-line-item-limit
- **PT-66** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Taxes and duties are not available on the Storefront cart; they are computed at checkout. CartCost.totalTaxAmount / totalDutyAmount (and *Estimated flags) are deprecated since 2025-01.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/changelog/tax-and-duties-are-deprecated-in-storefront-cart-api
- **PT-67** [confirmed, confidence medium] Duties/import taxes are collected as an estimate at checkout. Shipping: the cart only offers estimates (Cart.deliveryGroups; guide lists 'tax, and shipping estimates'); final shipping is selected at checkout. No page read states a single explicit 'shipping is calculated at X' rule.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/international/duties-and-import-taxes
- **PT-68** [confirmed, confidence medium] Draft order taxes default to the store's tax settings and the customer's shipping address; draft order currency/pricing follows the market associated with the order.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/fulfillment/managing-orders/create-orders/create-draft
- **PT-80** [confirmed, confidence high] Inventory policy enum ProductVariantInventoryPolicy has two values: CONTINUE ('Customers can buy this product variant after it's out of stock.') and DENY. Exposed as ProductVariant.inventoryPolicy in the Admin API.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/latest/enums/ProductVariantInventoryPolicy
- **PT-81** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Storefront ProductVariant stock fields: availableForSale, currentlyNotInStock (backorder indicator), quantityAvailable. quantityAvailable requires the unauthenticated_read_product_inventory scope.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest/objects/ProductVariant
- **PT-82** [partially_correct, design-critical, confidence high] cartCreate reports insufficient stock through `warnings` (not userErrors): MERCHANDISE_NOT_ENOUGH_STOCK ('The merchandise does not have enough stock.') and MERCHANDISE_OUT_OF_STOCK. `target` holds the affected CartLine ID. Documented example message: "The product 'Merchandise Title' is already sold out."
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/building-with-the-storefront-api/cart/cart-warnings
  **CORRECTION:** Warning codes, the `target` semantics and the quoted sentence are confirmed, but the documented example is a cartLinesAdd call where a previously added item went out of stock, not cartCreate; cartCreate behaviour is inferred from "All successful cart mutations can generate an array of CartWarning objects" and from CartCreatePayload having `warnings`. Two details were missed: (1) in the example the out-of-stock line is NOT removed - it stays in the cart with quantity 0, and the guide says "Clients can take the target from a warning and construct a call to CartLinesRemove to remove an out of stock item"; (2) the guide does not describe MERCHANDISE_NOT_ENOUGH_STOCK at all (enum description only), so whether quantity is clamped is undocumented on shopify.dev. The 2024-10 changelog page (shopify.dev/changelog/cart-warnings-in-storefront-api-cart) returned 404 for me as well. (https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/building-with-the-storefront-api/cart/cart-warnings)
- **PT-83** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence medium] Shopify's help center states the cart silently reduces quantity to available stock when a buyer asks for more than is in stock (so the checkout cart may hold fewer units than the vitrine cart).
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/checkout-settings/add-to-cart-limit
- **PT-84** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence medium] Add-to-cart limit: a merchant setting (Settings > Checkout > Advanced preferences), active by default, per variant; recommended default 50 for stores with fewer than 250 sales. It does not apply to POS carts, draft orders, B2B catalogs with quantity rules, variants with inventory tracking off, or 'continue selling when out of stock'. The page does not name Storefront API carts explicitly.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/checkout-settings/add-to-cart-limit
- **PT-85** [confirmed, confidence high] Quantity rules (QuantityRule: minimum, maximum, increment) are set in catalogs, apply per variant, and are re-validated in checkout. Cart userErrors codes: MINIMUM_NOT_MET, MAXIMUM_EXCEEDED ('The quantity must be below the specified maximum for the item.'), INVALID_INCREMENT.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/b2b/catalogs/quantity-pricing
- **PT-86** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] A Storefront cart holds at most 500 line items.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/building-with-the-storefront-api/cart
- **PT-87** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Any array input is capped at 250 entries, so a single cartCreate can carry at most 250 lines (CartInput.lines, discountCodes, giftCardCodes, attributes, metafields all max 250). Larger carts need cartLinesAdd follow-ups.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/usage/limits
- **PT-88** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Carts expire: unused/abandoned carts are removed within 30 days of creation, so a stored checkoutUrl is not durable.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/building-with-the-storefront-api/cart
- **PT-89** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Generic size/quantity error codes exist (CART_TOO_LARGE 'The cart is too large to save.', LESS_THAN 'The input value should be less than the maximum value allowed.') but no numeric platform-wide maximum quantity per line is documented.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest/enums/CartErrorCode
- **PT-90** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Checkout creation through the Storefront API is throttled per minute and signalled as HTTP 200 with a THROTTLED error; the number is not published. Buyer traffic otherwise has no fixed rate limit; tokenless access has a query complexity limit of 1,000.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest
- **PT-91** [confirmed, design-critical, confidence high] Server-side (middleware) Storefront calls should send the buyer's IP in the Shopify-Storefront-Buyer-IP header (case-sensitive, used with Shopify-Storefront-Private-Token); otherwise all buyers look like one client. Malicious-looking requests get '430 Shopify Security Rejection'.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest

## Design implications

- Method caveat: every quote was extracted through a fetch tool that summarizes pages with a small model. Reference-field quotes were mostly consistent across reads, but legal clauses (API Terms, ToS, AUP, Shopify Payments ToS) and their section numbers must be re-read on the live pages by a human before anyone relies on them. This research quotes terms and does not interpret them.
- Never forward vitrine prices (already the design). The checkout store prices each line from its own catalog for the buyer context, so always pass buyerIdentity.countryCode on cartCreate (and/or @inContext(country:)). Without context the result is the primary market in shop currency; an un-enabled country silently falls back to store currency.
- Price parity between vitrine and checkout store is not guaranteed by the platform. Documented divergence sources: live FX rates, the 1.5%/2% conversion fee baked into converted prices, non-customizable rounding, market percentage adjustments, dynamic tax-inclusive pricing, 'lowest price wins' across catalogs, and automatic/Functions discounts that exist on only one store.
- The most deterministic setup is: same store currency on both stores, same market/country structure, and fixed per-market prices (price-list fixed prices skip FX, rounding and tax-inclusive adjustments). The sync job should compare ProductVariant.contextualPricing(context) per country on both stores (2026-10 adds auditTrail) and flag mismatches in the mapping table.
- After cartCreate, read cart.cost (totalAmount, subtotalAmount) from the checkout store and compare with what the vitrine showed. If they differ, show the buyer the checkout-store amount before redirecting rather than letting checkout surprise them.
- If the checkout store does not use Shopify Payments or Adyen, every order is processed in its default currency, so the vitrine should display only that currency. Shopify Payments availability is country-specific (Brazil is not in the supported list read).
- Checkout can re-price after redirect when the shipping country differs from the cart's buyer country, and taxes/duties only exist at checkout. The middleware cannot guarantee the final total; it can only guarantee line prices for the stated country.
- Discounts are objects of the checkout store. Forward a code only if it exists there, then inspect cart.discountCodes[].applicable and warnings[] (DISCOUNT_NOT_FOUND etc.) in the cartCreate response; the mutation still succeeds, so treat warnings as first-class output. Respect the 5 product/order + 1 shipping code limit. Automatic discounts on the checkout store apply without input and cannot be suppressed per channel.
- Use the 2026-07+ discount fields (cart.discountApplications, lines[].discountAllocations(lineLevelOnly: false), sourceDiscountApplication); cart.discountAllocations is deprecated.
- Selling plans need their own vitrine->checkout mapping table (sellingPlanId), the unauthenticated_read_selling_plans scope and a supported gateway on the checkout store. The cart-permalink fallback cannot carry selling plans, so subscription lines must fail closed rather than fall back.
- Gift cards redeem only on the issuing store. Either sell/issue gift cards on the checkout store only, or exclude gift-card products and gift-card codes from the vitrine flow.
- Bundles: map the bundle parent variant, enable bundle publishing for the custom storefront channel on the checkout store, expect requiresComponents variants to be invisible on channels without bundle support, and handle MERCHANDISE_LINE_TRANSFORMERS_RUN_ERROR. Bundles and selling plans cannot be combined. lineUpdate price overrides need Plus.
- Draft orders are the only documented path that overrides price (priceOverride, custom line items, appliedDiscount). They need Admin API write_draft_orders, cap at 499 lines, do not reserve inventory by default and bypass the add-to-cart limit. Since the design requires prices to come from the checkout catalog, keep draft orders out of the buyer-controlled path; if ever used, compute everything server-side.
- Hard limits to code against: 250 lines per cartCreate call (array input cap), 500 lines per cart, carts expire within 30 days, add-to-cart limit per variant (on by default, recommended 50 on small stores), checkout creation throttled per minute (HTTP 200 + THROTTLED). Implement chunking via cartLinesAdd, backoff/retry, and only then the permalink fallback.
- Stock: with policy DENY the checkout cart may silently contain fewer units than the vitrine cart. Parse warnings (MERCHANDISE_NOT_ENOUGH_STOCK, MERCHANDISE_OUT_OF_STOCK, PRODUCT_UNAVAILABLE_IN_BUYER_LOCATION) and userErrors (MINIMUM_NOT_MET, MAXIMUM_EXCEEDED, INVALID_INCREMENT) and tell the buyer before redirecting.
- Server-side Storefront calls from the middleware should use the private token header plus Shopify-Storefront-Buyer-IP with the real buyer IP taken from the App Proxy request; otherwise all buyers are treated as one client (throttling, weaker bot protection) and requests may hit 430.
- Variant matching by barcode must move to the `barcodes` connection: ProductVariant.barcode is deprecated in Admin API 2026-10 and returns only the first entry.
- Platform terms: the design does use Shopify Checkout on the checkout store, and one owner may hold multiple stores. The quoted clauses that the design must be checked against by the merchant (ideally with legal advice or written confirmation from Shopify) are: API Terms 2.3(18)(i)-(ii) and the 'Pop-up Implementation' definition; ToS 1.8 and 4.5; AUP good-faith paragraph; Shopify Payments A16 (transactions must arise from your own sale; no intermediary/aggregator use; no working around checkout limitations), B2 (business name, billing descriptor and site URL shown to the buyer) and the covenants on bona fide sales and accurate description. There is no official statement on cross-store cart forwarding either way.
- Because the billing descriptor and site URL of the checkout store's payment account appear on the buyer's statement, the checkout store's registered business name, descriptor and product description should accurately match what the buyer saw on the vitrine; the Payments terms also require 30 days' notice before changing product types or trade name.
- Each store's custom app is single-merchant (API Terms 2.3(20)); keep one app per store owned by the same merchant, store client_id/client_secret encrypted, and do not share credentials beyond service providers acting on the merchant's behalf (2.1.5).

## Additional facts found by the verifier

- Storefront API carts work WITHOUT any access token. The API index lists tokenless features as "Products and Collections, Selling Plans, Search, Pages, Blogs, and Articles, Cart (read/write)" with "a query complexity limit of 1,000". Tokens are required for Product Tags, Metaobjects and Metafields, Menu, Customers; ProductVariant.quantityAvailable is marked "Token access required". Design impact: cartCreate on the checkout store may not need a stored Storefront token at all; a private token (header Shopify-Storefront-Private-Token plus Shopify-Storefront-Buyer-IP) is only needed for inventory reads, higher complexity and server-side buyer differentiation.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest
- On carts, buyerIdentity beats @inContext. "After a buyer identity is set with cartBuyerIdentityUpdate or during cartCreate, the buyer identity takes precedence" and conflicting values such as country are ignored. The design implication 'pass buyerIdentity.countryCode and/or @inContext(country:)' should become: always set CartInput.buyerIdentity.countryCode; use @inContext on cart mutations only for language, visitorConsent and channelId.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/building-with-the-storefront-api/in-context
- A checkout-store variant is only usable through the Storefront API if its product is published to the calling app's channel AND to the buyer's market: "the Storefront API will only return products that are published both to your sales channel and the market you're querying for"; "Unpublished products will behave just like they were archived or deleted". The catalog sync must therefore record, per mapped variant, publication to the middleware app's publication and to each market, not only SKU/barcode equality. The exact cartCreate error for an unpublished merchandiseId is not documented.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/storefront/latest/queries/product
- channelId scoping (2026-10): the changelog entry (July 15, 2026) says access is limited to channels created by the requesting API client and that when omitted the first channel owned by that client is used; the guide adds the channel must belong to the same store and app, else the error "Channel not found for the given id." Pricing and availability returned to the middleware are therefore those of the middleware app's own channel, which can differ from what the checkout store's Online Store channel shows.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/changelog/new-channelid-argument-for-incontext-directive-in-storefront-api-2026-10
- Exchange-rate refresh cadence IS documented: "Conversion rates are checked every 15 minutes, but typically updated only a few times a day." (Admin REST Currency resource, rate_updated_at). Two stores can therefore hold different rates for a short window even when configured identically; the parity check in the sync job should tolerate or detect this, or both stores should use fixed prices / manual rates.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-rest/latest/resources/currency
- Manual exchange rates are a second documented way to stabilise converted prices besides fixed price-list prices: "With manual exchange rates, you set a fixed exchange rate for each currency." The same page places rounding "after currency conversion and any price adjustments" and requires Shopify Payments or Adyen for multi-currency. The exchange-rates page states manual rates cannot be applied to the primary market.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/markets/customizations/local-currencies
- Out-of-stock lines are not removed for you. In the documented warning example the affected line stays in the cart with quantity 0, and the guide says "Clients can take the target from a warning and construct a call to CartLinesRemove to remove an out of stock item." After cartCreate the middleware must compare returned line quantities with requested ones, remove zero-quantity lines (or stop), and tell the buyer before redirecting to checkoutUrl.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/storefronts/headless/building-with-the-storefront-api/cart/cart-warnings
- Cart and checkout validation Functions installed on the checkout store also run against Storefront API carts: "Errors from validation functions are exposed to the Storefront API's Cart object"; the matching CartErrorCode is VALIDATION_CUSTOM ("Validation failed."). "You can activate a maximum of 25 validation functions on each store." The researcher's error handling list (MINIMUM_NOT_MET, MAXIMUM_EXCEEDED, INVALID_INCREMENT) omits this userError, which any validation app on the checkout store can raise.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/api/functions/latest/cart-and-checkout-validation
- Bundle publishing has concrete preconditions: disabled by default for custom storefronts; the app must be converted to a sales channel and the 'Sell Bundles' feature enabled in the Partner Dashboard; only product fixed bundles can be published, variant fixed bundles cannot. Exception quoted: "On admin-created custom apps, publishing bundles is enabled by default". The design uses Dev Dashboard apps, for which the default is not documented - test before promising bundle support.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/product-merchandising/bundles/turn-on-publishing
- Order attribution of carts created by the middleware: "Your app must be a channel app to use order attribution definitions." and "Use sourceName when you create a cart with the Storefront API." For permalinks, source_name plus a storefront access token (access_token parameter) is used. CartInput.sourceName exists in 2026-10. If the merchant wants checkout-store orders labelled by originating vitrine, this requires a channel app; otherwise use cart attributes/note.
  Source: https://shopify.dev/docs/apps/build/sales-channels/order-attribution
- API Terms contain a non-binding plain-language summary directly relevant to the checkout clause: "Use Shopify Checkout for payments and don't divert to any other web checkout or payment processing (unless you have our permission)." The page states "These values aren't legally binding." The binding text remains section 2.3's item on alternatives to Shopify Checkout and Pop-up Implementation (PT-100/101/102). No interpretation offered here.
  Source: https://www.shopify.com/legal/api-terms
- Shopify Plus expansion stores carry brand conditions the researcher did not report: they must "Be identical to the main brand with respect to store name and other branding" and "Carry the same types of goods and services as the main brand". A vitrine/checkout pair run as Plus expansion stores under different names or assortments would not meet the quoted conditions; the page points to a multi-brand agreement with separate Plus subscriptions instead.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/organization-settings/expansion-stores
- Multi-entity Shopify Payments (single store) detail: "Each entity has its own Shopify Payments account, payout currency, and bank account." No help.shopify.com or legal page was found that states whether one Shopify Payments account or one legal entity may serve several separate stores; the only material on that is community forum answers (not primary). Treat 'same entity, several stores, each with its own Shopify Payments account' as undocumented and confirm with Shopify Support in writing.
  Source: https://help.shopify.com/en/manual/payments/shopify-payments/onboarding/selling-with-multiple-entities

  Source: https://shopify.dev/changelog/release-notes/2026-07
